**Data loading:**
Same pattern as `generate_confusion_matrix_cv.py`: load fold test results from `fold_01..fold_05/test_results.json`, plus `ensemble_test_results_no_tta.json` and `ensemble_test_results_tta.json`.

Compute:
- Baseline mean/std for accuracy, f1_macro, f1_weighted from 5 folds
- Ensemble (no TTA) metrics from ensemble_soft_voting.metrics
- Ensemble+TTA metrics from ensemble_soft_voting.metrics
- Per-class F1 for baseline: average per_class[class]['f1-score'] across 5 folds
- Per-class F1 for ensemble+TTA: from ensemble_soft_voting.per_class
- TTA per-class deltas: from tta_delta_metrics.per_class_f1_delta

**Table 1: Overall Comparison**

Match the style from `5_3_resultados_clasificacion_CV.tex` (e.g., Table `tab:resultados_clasificacion_global`). Use `@{}lccc@{}` column format with booktabs.

Structure:
```latex
\begin{table}[htbp]
    \centering
    \caption{Comparación de rendimiento: Modelo individual (baseline) vs Ensemble con TTA.}
    \label{tab:comparison_ensemble_tta}
    \small
    \begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Métrica} & \textbf{Baseline Individual} & \textbf{Ensemble + TTA} & \textbf{Mejora} \\
        \midrule
        Exactitud (Accuracy) & 97.68\% $\pm$ 0.16\% & \textbf{98.26\%} & +0.58 pp \\
        F1-Score Macro & 96.47\% $\pm$ 0.27\% & \textbf{97.12\%} & +0.65 pp \\
        F1-Score Ponderado & 97.67\% $\pm$ 0.16\% & \textbf{98.25\%} & +0.58 pp \\
        \midrule
        Muestras evaluadas & 1{,}895 $\times$ 5 & 1{,}895 & --- \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}
```

All numeric values must be computed from the JSON files, NOT hardcoded. Format percentages to 2 decimal places. The "Mejora" column shows the delta in percentage points (pp). Use `\textbf{}` to bold the ensemble+TTA values. Use `{,}` for thousands separator in LaTeX.

**Table 2: Per-Class TTA Impact**

```latex
\begin{table}[htbp]
    \centering
    \caption{Impacto del ensemble con TTA por categoría diagnóstica.}
    \label{tab:tta_per_class_impact}
    \small
    \begin{tabular}{@{}lcccc@{}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Categoría} & \textbf{F1 Baseline} & \textbf{F1 Ensemble+TTA} & \textbf{$\Delta$ F1} & \textbf{Impacto} \\
        \midrule
        COVID-19 & XX.XX\% & \textbf{XX.XX\%} & +X.XX pp & Mejora \\
        Normal & XX.XX\% & \textbf{XX.XX\%} & +X.XX pp & Mejora \\
        Neumonía Viral & XX.XX\% & XX.XX\% & $-$X.XX pp & Leve degradación \\
        \midrule
        \multicolumn{5}{l}{\small Impacto a nivel de caso: 6 mejorados, 3 perjudicados, 1{,}886 neutrales (neto: +3)} \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}
```

For the "Impacto" column:
- If delta > 0.1pp: "Mejora"
- If delta is between -0.1pp and +0.1pp: "Neutro"
- If delta < -0.1pp: "Leve degradación"

The case-level impact line (helped/hurt/neutral) comes from `ensemble_test_results_tta.json` → `case_level_analysis.summary`.

**IMPORTANT formatting details (match existing thesis .tex exactly):**
- Use `\%` for percent signs in LaTeX
- Use `$\pm$` for plus-minus
- Use `$\Delta$` for delta symbol
- Use `$-$` for negative sign in math mode (not text hyphen)
- Use `\textbf{}` for bold values
- Use `{,}` inside numbers for thousands separator (LaTeX convention)
- Use `pp` for percentage points
- DO NOT use pandas.to_latex() — write the LaTeX directly as strings to have full control over formatting, matching the existing thesis table style exactly. The existing tables in `5_3_resultados_clasificacion_CV.tex` use hand-crafted LaTeX, not pandas output.
